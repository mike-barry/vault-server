# Widget Assembly

Plans the assembly of the widget for the [[Widget]] project. Part specifications live in [[Gizmo-Reference]]; fastener torque values come from [[Gizmo-Reference#Torque Specs|the torque table]].

## Goals

- Assemble one working widget from a quorvium alloy gizmo and off-the-shelf fasteners

## Target Design

The gizmo mounts to the base plate with four M3 fasteners torqued per [[Resources/Gizmo-Reference]]. Alignment is verified against the housing before final tightening.
